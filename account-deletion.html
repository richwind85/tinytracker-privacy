Account & Data Deletion Policy

App Name: Tiny Tracker

Developer: Windsor Studios

At Tiny Tracker, we respect your privacy and provide simple mechanisms to delete your account and associated data.

How to Delete Your Account and Data Within the App

  1. Open the Tiny Tracker app on your Android device.
  2. Go to the More tab or Account settings.
  3. Tap on Sign Out or delete your household / account data. All local data stored on your device will be cleared immediately.

How to Request Complete Cloud Data Deletion

If you have signed in with Google and wish to have all your cloud data (including household logs and profile data stored in Firebase Firestore) permanently deleted from our servers, you can:

  • Send an email to our support team at richwind85@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Include the email address associated with your Google account.
  • We will process your request and permanently delete all associated cloud records within 7 business days.

Data Retention

Upon receiving a deletion request, all personal identifiers, logs, and household records are permanently removed from our active database.
