Privacy Policy for Tiny Tracker

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Windsor Studios ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Tiny Tracker mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.
1. Information We Collect
A. Information You Provide Directly

    Child Profile Data: Information you choose to input to track sleep and wake windows, such as child names/nicknames, ages, and logged sleep schedules.

    User Input: Any logs, notes, or entries created within the application.

B. Automatically Collected Information

    Device Data: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers to ensure performance and compatibility.

    Usage Statistics: Basic app analytics (such as feature usage and crash reports) to improve performance and stability.

C. Third-Party Services & APIs

    Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, updates, and core platform functions.

    Gemini API / AI Processing: Text or log data submitted for sleep pattern analysis or AI features is processed via secure API endpoints in accordance with Google AI data handling policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

    To provide, maintain, and optimize the functionality of Tiny Tracker.

    To generate sleep recommendations and wake-window estimations.

    To diagnose technical issues, fix bugs, and release app updates.

    To comply with legal obligations and Google Play Developer policies.

3. Data Storage and Retention

    Local Storage: The primary tracking logs and sleep data are stored locally on your device wherever possible.

    Data Retention: We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or your child's data to third parties or advertisers. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

    Service Providers: With trusted third-party providers (such as Google infrastructure and analytics tools) strictly to operate the app.

    Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Children's Privacy

Tiny Tracker is a tool designed for parents and caregivers to log baby sleep and care data.

    The app does not intentionally collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

    All data regarding children must be entered by a parent or legal guardian.

6. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
7. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

    Access, update, or delete the personal data held about you.

    Clear your locally stored app data at any time by clearing the app cache/data in your Android device settings or uninstalling the application.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.
